Client Diagnostic Checklist

The 3-pillar account health assessment: 23 checks across Targeting & Data Quality, Marketing Effectiveness, and Sales Operations & Engagement — each with the question to ask, the healthy signal, the red flag, and the benchmark. Informed by 367 Knowledge Gap sessions. Formerly the intranet tool /tools/client-diagnostic, retired 2026-08-06; this page and its markdown source are now the source of truth.

Checklist vs. playbook — two instruments, two triggers

§1
InstrumentTriggerQuestion it answers
This checklistClient health review, QBR, at-risk escalation, engagement call prepAcross the three controllable drivers, where is this account unhealthy?
Diagnostic playbookA specific KPI declined (Gross Leads or a GNACC conversion)Which hypothesis explains this specific break, and what test confirms it?

The checklist is a breadth-first sweep — assess every item, mark it Healthy / Watch / Issue, leave with a prioritized action list. The playbook is depth-first — route one broken KPI to its hypothesis branch. A checklist "Issue" often becomes the entry point into a playbook branch.

How to run it

  1. Read the market context signals first — external conditions inform, but do not score, the diagnosis.
  2. Walk the three pillars in order. For every item: ask the client the question, compare against the healthy signal and red flag, mark Healthy / Watch / Issue.
  3. Build the executive summary: healthy/watch/issue counts, priority issues first.
  4. For every priority issue, run the five-step diagnostic sequence before prescribing anything.

§6.1Recommended diagnostic sequence — same for every priority issue

  1. Segment — break by geography, property type, and price tier. Aggregates hide the truth: a client may be fine in County A and failing in County B.
  2. Compare — current vs historical (YoY, QoQ, MoM). New problems point to recent changes; ongoing trends point to structural issues.
  3. Benchmark — client vs top 25% of active 8020REI clients in similar markets. If top performers thrive in the same conditions, the market isn't the issue.
  4. Correlate — which leading indicator changed first? Did engagement calls drop before deal flow? Did BuyBox updates stop before hit rates declined?
  5. Prioritize — fix the biggest bottleneck first. A 2x improvement on a 50% conversion rate matters more than a 2x improvement on a 2% rate.

This is the checklist-flavored cousin of the playbook's SAE loop — Segment → Isolate → Explore. Same first move: segment before believing any aggregate.
